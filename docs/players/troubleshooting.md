# Troubleshooting

Most support requests move faster when they include the right evidence.

Avoid posting private tokens, secrets, or personal screenshots in public channels.

## Useful Evidence

- The affected Citizen iD page URL.
- The UTC time when the problem happened.
- The request ID shown on an error page.
- The provider involved, such as Discord, Google, Twitch, or RSI.
- The community or Discord server where the problem appears.
- A short description of what you expected and what happened instead.

## Common Areas To Check

Check whether a maintenance message is active.

Check whether you need to accept a legal agreement before continuing.

Check whether your account is RSI-verified when a role or application requires verification.

Check whether your privacy settings allow the lookup a community or application is trying to perform.

Check whether you recently hit a rate limit and need to wait before retrying.
