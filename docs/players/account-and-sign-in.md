# Account Sign-Up And Sign-In

Citizen iD supports Discord, Google, and Twitch sign-in in production.

Staging environments may expose fewer providers while integrations are being tested.

## Account Entry Points

Use the Citizen iD sign-up page when you are creating an account for the first time.

Use the Citizen iD sign-in page when you already have an account or are returning from a community tool.

You may also encounter Citizen iD through a community application that redirects you to sign in and approve requested access.

## Linked Providers

Provider accounts help Citizen iD identify you and support platform-specific features.

Discord is especially important for Discord linked roles, bot features, and community server automation.

Google and Twitch can also be used for account sign-in where they are enabled.

## After Sign-Up

After sign-up, review your account dashboard.

Link any providers you expect to use.

Verify your RSI account if a community or application requires verified Star Citizen identity.

Review your privacy settings before relying on public profile discovery.
