# Player Guide

Citizen iD lets Star Citizen players use one trusted identity across community tools and Discord servers.

This guide covers the player side of the platform.

It explains how to create an account, sign in with Discord, Google, or Twitch, verify an RSI account, claim Discord linked roles, review authorized apps, and control privacy settings.

## Start Here

- [Account Sign-Up And Sign-In](/players/account-and-sign-in) explains the account entry points and supported providers.
- [RSI Verification](/players/rsi-verification) explains the short-bio verification flow and why verification may be required.
- [Discord Linked Roles](/players/discord-linked-roles) explains how Citizen iD connects your account to Discord role claims.
- [Authorized Apps](/players/authorized-apps) explains consent, authorizations, and revocation.
- [Privacy And Data](/players/privacy-and-data) explains public profile discovery, external-account discovery, analytics consent, data export, and deletion status.
- [Troubleshooting](/players/troubleshooting) lists the evidence support needs when something is blocked or out of sync.

## What Citizen iD Does For Players

Citizen iD reduces repeated verification work.

You can verify your RSI account once and then use that identity with communities and tools that trust Citizen iD.

You stay in control through explicit application consent, revocation, linked account management, and public discovery settings.

## What Citizen iD Does Not Do

Citizen iD does not give every community or application all of your account data automatically.

Applications request scopes, and you approve the access they need.

Discord role automation only works where a community has configured the Citizen iD bot or Discord linked roles.

Account deletion is currently request-only.

Self-service deletion is intended, and the docs will call that out when the product flow becomes available.
