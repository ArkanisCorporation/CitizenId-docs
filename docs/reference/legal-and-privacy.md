# Legal And Privacy

Citizen iD legal documents are public application surfaces.

They include terms of service, privacy policy, cookie notice, provider list, developer terms, and staff terms where applicable.

## Player-Facing Privacy

Players should understand public discovery, external-account discovery, analytics consent, data export, and account deletion status.

See [Privacy Controls](/players/privacy-controls) and [Data Rights](/players/data-rights).

## Developer Terms

Developers request the Integrator product role before managing applications.

Developer terms acceptance is part of that request flow.

See [Get Access](/community-developers/access).

## Required Agreements

Citizen iD can redirect authenticated users to accept required legal agreements before continuing.

Optional notices can be informational and may not require explicit agreement.

The detailed legal document text remains in the Citizen iD application.
