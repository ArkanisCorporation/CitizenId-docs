# Support Evidence

Good support reports are specific and safe.

Never share access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, password reset links, or private account screenshots in public channels.

## Everyone

- A short summary of the issue.
- The affected page or tool.
- The UTC timestamp.
- The request ID from the error page when available.
- The provider involved, such as Discord, Google, Twitch, RSI, or Citizen iD.

## Players

- Your Citizen iD account link when safe to share.
- The community or Discord server where the issue appears.
- Whether your RSI account is verified.
- Whether the issue involves privacy settings, linked roles, or authorized apps.

## Community Admins

- The community slug.
- The Discord server involved.
- The role or nickname template involved.
- The role-assignment audit entry or operation ID when available.
- Whether manual resync was attempted.

## Developers

- The client ID or application name.
- The environment, such as production or staging.
- The OAuth flow, endpoint, and scope set involved.
- The exact non-secret error response.
- Whether the request used a user token, application token, or integrator-user session.
