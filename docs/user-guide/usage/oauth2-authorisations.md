# Understanding Authorisations

When you log in to an application using Citizen iD, you grant that application an "authorisation" to access parts of your account data.
These authorisations are based on **Scopes**.

## What is a Scope?

A scope is a specific permission category. Common scopes include:

- **Profile**: Access to your display name and avatar.
- **RSI Profile**: Access to your verified RSI handle and selected profile data.
- **RSI Organizations**: Access to your public RSI organization memberships.
- **Email**: Access to your email address.
- **Offline Access**: The ability to access your data even when you are not actively using the application (background sync),
- _and more_.


## Viewing Your Authorisations

You can view all applications you have authorised in your [Citizen iD Authorized Apps](https://citizenid.space/account/apps).
This list shows:
- The name of the application.
- The date you first authorised it.
- The specific permissions (scopes) it has access to.
