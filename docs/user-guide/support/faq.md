# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

**Q: Is Citizen iD affiliated with Cloud Imperium Games (CIG)?**
<br>A: No. Citizen iD is an independent community project and is not endorsed by or affiliated with CIG or RSI.

**Q: Is it free to use?**
<br>A: Yes, Citizen iD is free for both users and integrators.

## Privacy & Data

**Q: What data do you store?**
<br>A: We store your Discord ID, your verified RSI handle, and any organization data needed to provide our services. We do not store passwords.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
<br>A: Yes. You can fully delete your account and all associated data from the "Account Settings" page in your dashboard.

## Verification

**Q: Why do I need to change my RSI bio code?**
<br>A: This is a standard method to prove you own the account. It is temporary, and you can change it back immediately after verification is complete.

**Q: What happens if I change my RSI handle?**
<br>A: Our system will detect the change and automatically revoke your verification to prevent impersonation. You will need to re-verify with your new handle.
