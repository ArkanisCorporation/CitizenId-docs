Integrator Registration ​

To get your Citizen iD OAuth2 credentials:

  1. Create an account on the Citizen iD website
    • Make sure to verify and link your Discord account before proceeding
  2. Contact the Citizen iD staff via the official community Discord
    • Use the #support-and-contact channel on the server
  3. After a short dialog and on-boarding you'll receive access to the developer portal
    • Initially on the staging environment,
    • and after an internal review of your application, the production one.
